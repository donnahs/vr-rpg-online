🏗️

Procedural Dungeons

Every run is different. 5-8 connected rooms with enemies, loot, and secrets.

🕶️

WebXR VR Support

Play in your browser with any VR headset. No download needed.

👥

Multiplayer

Team up or compete with friends in real-time dungeon runs.

⚔️

Skill-Based Combat

Melee attacks, dodging, potions, and equipment upgrades.

  • Node.js — Authoritative game server
